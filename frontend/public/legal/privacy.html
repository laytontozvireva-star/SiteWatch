SiteWatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy describes how SiteWatch may handle information when you use its website monitoring service and optional Vercel integration.

Information that may be processed

  • Account information needed to provide the service.
  • Website and project details that users submit or connect.
  • Error reports, monitoring events, alert information, and related technical metadata.
  • Vercel account, project, and deployment information made available through permissions you grant.
  • Technical and diagnostic information needed to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the service.

How information is used

Information may be used to operate monitoring features, display errors and deployment activity, deliver alerts, maintain service reliability, troubleshoot problems, and protect the service from misuse.

Vercel integration

If you connect Vercel, SiteWatch processes information available through the permissions you authorize and any configured integration events. You should review the permissions shown during authorization before approving access. You can discontinue using the integration and revoke its access through the relevant account settings where available.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers needed to operate SiteWatch. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service, its users, or others. This policy does not mean that every listed data category is collected in every use of the service.

Retention and security

Information is retained as needed for service operation, legitimate business purposes, and applicable legal requirements. Actual retention periods depend on the data and system configuration. SiteWatch uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and questions

You can stop using the service and disconnect integrations where supported. For privacy questions or requests concerning your information, contact laytontozvireva@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before handling a request.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as SiteWatch develops. An updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
